## What's new

- **Photo scenery where AutoOrtho's mesh has gaps.** The regions on the **Packages** page come from AutoOrtho's collection, and it is missing a few tiles: 9 in Oregon, around Portland and along the coast, and one in Tonga. Until now, those places had no photo scenery. OrthoStreamXP now keeps these tiles in its own collection and adds them to **North America** and **Australia and the Pacific**. There is no extra package to install.
- **Ground detail starts at 30 %.** The **Ground detail** slider in **Settings › Quality** now goes from 30 % to 100 %, and 30 % is the default. At 30 %, X-Plane's grass and dirt pattern is faint, and the photos stay clean and sharp. **Off** is no longer a choice.

## Good to know

- **Already have North America or Australia and the Pacific?** The missing tiles are added by themselves when you open OrthoStreamXP after the update. A notice on **Home**, **Adding the tiles AutoOrtho lacks**, shows the progress, and **Tiles AutoOrtho lacks were added** tells you when it is done. North America downloads about 225 MB, and Australia and the Pacific about 2 MB (a little less without roads, forests and buildings).
- You can fly meanwhile. If OrthoStreamXP is streaming while the tiles are added, X-Plane sees them from the next start of OrthoStreamXP.
- If the download fails, nothing else changes, and the tiles are added at a later start. Until then, the region on the **Packages** page shows **Update available** with the number of tiles. **Update** downloads only those tiles, and it works even while OrthoStreamXP runs.
- Regions you install from now on get these tiles as part of the install. They never replace a file of AutoOrtho's, and your own scenery packages are not changed.
- **If your Ground detail was Off** (the former default), it is now 30 %. A value of 30 % or more stays as it was. Like every setting, it can be changed only while OrthoStreamXP is stopped, and applies from the next start.
