## What's new

- **Twice as sharp, from the same downloads.** Until now, many textures were shrunk after the photos were downloaded, so part of the detail you downloaded was thrown away. Now every texture keeps all the detail of its photos. Away from airports, every preset is twice as sharp as before: Balanced 2048 px, Sharp 4096 px, and Ultra and Extra 8192 px. With Extra, the textures near airports are twice as sharp too (8192 px from zoom 19). The imagery and the downloads are the same as before.
- **Crisper photos.** Each new texture is sharpened a little as it is built, so roofs, field edges and treetops look crisper, without bright or dark outlines. The photos also get a little more contrast as X-Plane reads them, because aerial photos are often hazy. Both are always on and have no setting. The **Colours** preview shows the contrast in its **After** picture.
- **Smoother flights into new scenery.** When X-Plane loads the next scenery tile in flight, it reads a small version of every photo in that tile and waits for each one. Near airports a tile has hundreds of them, and that wait showed up as stutters. OrthoStreamXP now gets those photos ready up to five minutes ahead, along your path, so X-Plane finds them ready when it gets there.
- **Less memory.** OrthoStreamXP keeps less in RAM and leaves more for X-Plane. For each scenery package, it keeps at most 512 MB of textures ready for X-Plane, down from 1 GB. Downloaded photos stay compressed until they are needed. Between texture builds, it keeps the memory of only one build, instead of up to 1 GB.
- **Faster loading.** OrthoStreamXP now packs the photos into textures with Intel's ISPC texture compressor, about six times faster than before. That leaves much more of the processor to X-Plane, and flights with an empty cache load sooner. Water and other even areas also keep their colour more exactly.
- **Satellite seasons.** A new choice for **Season** in **Settings › Colours**: **Satellite**. The calendar colours every place the same way: green in spring, brown in winter. But a winter on the Atlantic or Mediterranean coast stays green, a dry summer turns the fields to straw, and in the tropics the dry season comes when the calendar says nothing happens. With Satellite, the grass and fields look as green as NASA's satellites saw that place at that time of the last year, compared with its greenest month. Trees keep the calendar, and towns, rock and sand keep their colours.
- **X-Plane Custom Scenery page.** A new page in the sidebar shows X-Plane's scenery list (`scenery_packs.ini`), highest priority first. Switch packages on or off, and drag them up or down to change which one covers the others. There is no need to edit the file by hand. The packages OrthoStreamXP adds are marked, so you can see where the photo scenery sits.
- **Switch a package off on Home.** Each package under **Scenery packages** on the Home page has its own switch. Leave out a region you are not flying today, or a package you want to test without, and keep it in the folder. It applies from the next start.
- **A bigger cache map.** The map on the **Cache** page now uses the full width of the window. Scroll to zoom and drag to move it. The buttons zoom in, zoom out and show all your areas, and the latitude and longitude lines help you find a place.
- **Colour preview in sight.** On the **Colours** tab, the Before and After preview stays at the top while you scroll to the settings below, so you can see each change as you make it.

## Why flights load faster

When a flight starts, X-Plane asks for thousands of textures at once. For each one, OrthoStreamXP downloads the photos and then packs them into the texture format X-Plane reads. That packing took a large share of the processor during the load, just when X-Plane needs it most.

The new compressor does the same work about six times faster. In cold-start tests over the same scene of about 3,000 textures, with an empty cache, X-Plane finished loading the photo scenery between a quarter and almost half sooner, and the longest wait for a single texture was halved or better. The download speed also differed between those runs, so how much you gain depends on your processor and your connection. Places already in the cache load from it as before.

## Fixes

- When OrthoStreamXP stops, it now takes out of `scenery_packs.ini` the packages it added there at the start. X-Plane no longer lists packages that are only there while OrthoStreamXP runs.
- **Distant scenery has the same colours as near.** The farther away the ground was, the darker it got, most of all towards the horizon. And far scenery was made from Bing's coarsest pictures, which are other, lighter and yellower imagery than its detailed photos. Distant ground now keeps the brightness and colours of the photos you see up close, so there is no lighter, yellower ring where far scenery meets near.
- A photo tile that no imagery provider has no longer leaves its texture without its picture: the gap is filled from a coarser photo of the same place.

## Good to know

- **Your first flight in each area downloads its photos again.** The new texture sizes go in a new cache folder for each preset, so every area is built again, sharper. From the second flight, the area comes from the cache as before.
- Your textures from 1.4 are not used any more. On the **Cache** page, under **By quality**, they are a second row for your preset, showing the old sizes. Delete that row to free the space.
- The bigger textures take more room in the cache and more graphics memory. The preset cards in **Settings › Quality** show how much graphics memory the photo textures take, and warn you if your graphics card is short. Until you have flown at the new sizes, that figure is an estimate. If X-Plane stutters or turns blurry, try one preset lower.
- Smoother flights into new scenery needs X-Plane to send the aircraft's position. On the same PC it does this by itself.
- Your season setting is not changed by the update. To try Satellite, turn on **Colour the scenery for the season** and choose **Satellite** in **Settings › Colours** while OrthoStreamXP is stopped.
- Satellite downloads about 2 MB once a year for each area of about 500 km, the first time you fly there. Until then, or without an internet connection, that area uses the calendar. The colour preview shows only what is already downloaded.
- The satellites see the land at about 4 km, so a whole area changes colour together, not each field on its own.
- The X-Plane Custom Scenery page and the package switches on Home can be changed only while OrthoStreamXP is stopped. X-Plane reads the scenery list when it starts, so a change shows from its next start.
- If you switch off every region and leave only an overlay package (roads, forests and buildings) on, OrthoStreamXP will not start: overlays need a region's ground to draw on. The notice on Home tells you to switch a region back on under **Scenery packages**.
- **Even out the colours between photos** has been removed from **Settings › Colours**. If you had it on, the photos are now shown as downloaded, with your season and exposure settings as before. Your settings file still loads as it is.
