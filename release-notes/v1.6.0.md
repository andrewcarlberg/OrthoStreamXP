## What's new

- **Airports page: flatten an airport.** Photo scenery comes with its own, more detailed ground, and some airports end up on a bump or in a hollow of it: a sloping runway, a terminal on a hill, aircraft parked in a dip. A new **Airports** page in the sidebar fixes that one airport at a time. Search by ICAO code, name or city, and click **Flatten**: X-Plane then levels the ground under that airport. **Undo** puts it back as it was.
- Without a search, the page lists the airports you have flattened. Each one says where its layout comes from, for example *from Global Airports*, and whether the flattening is in effect.

## How flattening works

OrthoStreamXP does not change any airport's own files. It puts a copy of the airport, marked to be flattened, in a scenery package of its own, **OrthoStreamXP Flattened Airports** in X-Plane's `Custom Scenery` folder, and lists it just above the airport's own scenery so X-Plane uses the copy. **Undo** takes the copy out again, and undoing the last airport removes the package. **Open the folder** shows the package in Explorer.

## Good to know

- **X-Plane reads the airports when it starts**, so a flattened airport shows level from X-Plane's next start.
- You can flatten an airport while OrthoStreamXP is stopped or while it runs, but not while it is starting or stopping. You do not need to restart OrthoStreamXP.
- **Use it for X-Plane's own airports, not for custom airport scenery.** A custom airport loses its buildings and other scenery when the flattened copy is above it. In X-Plane's scenery list, keep **OrthoStreamXP Flattened Airports** below your custom airports. You can check its place on the **X-Plane Custom Scenery** page.
- **Already flattened by its own scenery** means the airport is level already: there is nothing to do.
- If an airport's layout changes later, for example after an X-Plane update, the page says **Flattened, but its scenery changed since**. Click **Flatten again** to take the new layout.
- If the page says X-Plane uses another package's copy, the flattened package is lower in the scenery list or switched off. **Flatten again** puts it back in place.
- Nothing is downloaded, and none of your settings change with the update.
- **Reporting a problem?** The GitHub issue page now has forms: **Bug report**, **Scenery looks wrong** and **Feature request**. They ask for what helps most, such as the log and the exact place.
